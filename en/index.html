BTC at its lowest since July

Bitcoin fell toward $61,300 on Thursday evening, just after Anthropic’s stock-market debut and the drop in tech stocks, its lowest since early July. More than $600 million in leveraged bets were liquidated in 24 hours. It is down about 13% over one week (CoinMarketCap).

Sources: CoinDesk ↗, Chosun Biz ↗

Bitcoin ETFs: −$582 million in one session

A third straight day of outflows on 19 November: −$582.3 million, of which −$541.1 million for BlackRock’s IBIT, almost double the previous day’s −$306.5 million. Over November, outflows are approaching $2.9 billion.

Sources: Farside Investors ↗, Gate News ↗, crypto.news ↗

Ether slips back under $1,750

AMD −7.7% after a Hunterbrook attack

Short seller Hunterbrook says recent AMD firmware is showing up in servers from the Chinese firm IEIT, on the US blacklist since March 2025. AMD denies it and says it complies with export rules; no violation has been established.

Apple is preparing its own AI agent

Apple job listings describe an agent able to browse, use apps and write code. According to Bloomberg, the new boss John Ternus is pressing Craig Federighi to speed up; an “agent” Siri could arrive as soon as 2027.

Robotics: visas denied to Chinese engineers for CES

The United States is refusing visas to technicians from Chinese robot makers, including Xpeng and Engine AI, ahead of the January CES. One of them withdrew after its 15 engineers were refused; marketing teams are generally getting through.

Sixth straight decline for the S&P 500

The indexes plunged the moment Anthropic started trading: profit-taking on AI, Nvidia −3.3%. The Dow, which lost 1,101 points, closes under 50,000 for the first time since June. The Nasdaq falls to its lowest in seven months.

Sources: CNBC ↗, Reuters via Euronext ↗, AP ↗

Tokyo loses more than 4% this morning

The Nikkei 225 falls about 4.5% to around 56,775 points, its lowest in seven months, pulled down by Advantest, Tokyo Electron and SoftBank. All of Asia is following Wall Street.

Sources: AP ↗, The Mainichi ↗

Philadelphia industry brakes hard

The Philadelphia Fed manufacturing index falls from 20.9 to 6.3 in November, its lowest since May; orders move to −0.4. Prices paid rise to 58.2: activity is slowing, but costs are not.

Sources: Philadelphia Fed ↗, Investing.com ↗

Oil steady despite Hormuz

Brent finishes at $91.98 (+0.03%) and WTI at $83.21, its lowest since 26 August: shipping incidents at Hormuz offset the rise in US inventories. For the IEA, the war has already taken 2.8 billion barrels off the market.

Sources: Reuters via Euronext ↗, Rigzone (Bloomberg) ↗
